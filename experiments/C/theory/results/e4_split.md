# E4: location-calibrated split test of reported real poles

Per reported real pole z of the `foh_eivo` report: a real pole added at w = f z, f ∈ (0.25, 0.4, 0.63, 1.6, 2.5, 4.0), everything else refitted; gain G(w) against 16 EIV draws, ratio = G / (κ_w χ²_2(0.99)), κ_w = max(1, mean G* / 2). Flagged if max ratio > 1. Split fit: the flagged pole's split model refitted with w free. Null `denoise`: draws from the report's fit; `bc`: from its bias-corrected fit. Checks without refits: dup = a reported pole within 10 % of another, alias = a complex pole above the Nyquist frequency.
